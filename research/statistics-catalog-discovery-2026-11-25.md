# Statistics catalog discovery: 2026-11-25

## Discovery and screening

The API pass collected **855 raw entries**, **764 unique leads**, and **670 leads not matched by the automated title/DOI check**. These are paper or repository leads, not verified paper-data pairs. Targeted journal, package, and archive checks also used the Journal of Statistical Software, the CRAN Spatial Task View, journal supplements, author GitHub repositories, arXiv, OSF, Zenodo, 4TU.ResearchData, and NASA Open Data.

Keyword themes: geostatistics and kriging, spatio-temporal models, spatial point processes, hierarchical forecast reconciliation, statistical process control, factorial and optimal designs, and environmental extremes. Journal-index queries covered Journal of Statistical Software, The R Journal, International Journal of Forecasting, and Spatial Statistics; the International Journal of Forecasting query and two Crossref keyword queries returned HTTP 429. The independent source check did verify International Journal of Forecasting papers.

The two verification passes documented at least **33 targeted reviews** (26 accepted, 2 dataset-verified duplicates, and 5 held below), separate from automated title/DOI screening. **28 had clear data sources; 26 were added.** A selective deep review and the need to establish the paper-to-data relationship and actual access, beyond search metadata, kept the batch below 50. Publisher inventories, repository trees, direct file downloads, archive listings, and named public dataset documentation were used as appropriate; analysis code was not executed.

- Held: the [Pearl River precipitation archive](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.29209829.v1) has downloadable data, but its associated publication was not confirmed; the [spatio-temporal Bayesian extremes paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02618) did not yield a verified link to its application data in this pass.
- Held: the [archive for Why Experimenters Might Not Always Want to Randomize](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/I5KCWI) lists MATLAB scripts and a README without a separately verified dataset.

No official or candidate label was added, changed, or promoted; `LABEL_CANDIDATES` remains empty.
