# Statistics catalog discovery: 2026-11-27

## Discovery and screening

The API and journal-index pass collected **823 raw entries**, **752 unique leads**, and **648 leads not matched by the automated title/DOI check**. These are discovery counts, not verified paper-data pairs. A separate design, survey, and multivariate shortlist logged 27 distinct leads, nine already cataloged and 18 taken to source-level review. It was not deduplicated against the API pool, so it is reported separately.

The search rotated through changepoints, intermittent demand, hierarchical and probabilistic forecasting, time-series classification, statistical process control, compositional analysis, and experimental design. Some Crossref queries were rate limited, and several API calls timed out. Verification used publisher supplements and tables, arXiv, JSS and CRAN package data, author repositories, a Zenodo record, Euro foreign exchange data from the ECB, the OECD PISA catalog, and the M5 competition organizers' inventory.

Across both passes, **34 leads received detailed review**. **25 had clear data sources**: 23 were added and two were already cataloged. Nine were not accepted in this pass. The batch stayed below 50 because the rest of the initial pool did not receive full verification and some promising leads had incomplete or unconfirmed access. A file inventory, named package object, or documented access route is distinguished from a direct file check. Published numeric tables were accepted where they contain the observed design and responses. Analysis code was not executed.

## Whole-catalog label health

Ran `relabel-catalog.mjs` and then `assess-labels.mjs`. The relabeler assigned existing `mixture models` to the ECB exchange-rate paper and `longitudinal data analysis` to the existing multiwave workfare record. No official or candidate label was created, changed, or promoted; `LABEL_CANDIDATES` remains empty. All 23 additions are marked `auto-discovered` and `needs-review`; 13 with fewer than three supported labels also carry `needs-label-review`.

The assessor reports **2,370 records and 82 active official labels**, with no unknown or underused labels, registry hierarchy issues, or unflagged label-count violations. The additions grew time series analysis (+10), high-dimensional statistics (+7, often inherited from dimension reduction), dimension reduction (+6), and change point detection and time series forecasting (+5 each). Every addition has its direct official parents.

The direct-assignment audit found **1,024 nonempty label pairs**, up from 1,021. The near-identical survival models and survival analysis filters (Jaccard 0.97), four existing normalized-title groups with duplicates, 14 older records lacking topics, and 323 missing direct-parent assignments across 313 older records were unchanged.
